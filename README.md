📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI Project
📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer distribution, and product-related metrics using Power BI.
I cleaned and prepared the dataset, analyzed sales trends, and developed an interactive dashboard to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.
The dashboard provides an overview of revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), profit, and customer metrics to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyzed overall revenue and profitability.
Identified monthly revenue trends.
Compared profit contributions across brands.
Examined profitability across customer income levels.
Analyzed quantity distribution by product color.
Monitor customer distribution across months.

🛠️ Tools Used
Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning and preparation.
Power BI: Data analysis and visualization.
📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard highlights the following metrics:
Total Revenue=$3M
Total Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)=$2M
Total Profit=$932k
Total Customers=250

🔍 Key Insights
1. Monthly Revenue Analysis
The dashboard shows the following monthly revenue figures:
January: $1.035M
February: $1.025M
March: $1.047M
Insight: March recorded the highest revenue, while February experienced a slight decline compared with January. Revenue remained relatively stable across the three months.
2. Profit by Customer Income Level
The profit distribution across  High, Medium and low income levels 
Medium income level generated $347k,High income level generated $302k,Low income level generated $283k
Insight:
Medium-income customers generated the highest profit, followed by high-income and low-income customers.
3. Quantity by Product Color
The dashboard displays the following quantities purchased by product color:
White=6.1K
Gray=5.2K
Black=4.3K
Insight: White products recorded the highest quantity, followed by gray and black products. This may help the business understand product
preferences and plan inventory accordingly.
4. Brand Profitability
The profit contributions by brand were:
Apple=$197K
Lenovo=$160K
Samsung=$160K
HP=$148K
Asus=$142K
Dell=$126K
Insight: Apple generated the highest profit among the brands displayed, while Dell recorded the lowest. Lenovo and Samsung generated equal
profits of $160K each.
6. Customer Distribution by Month
The dashboard displays the following monthly customer percentages:
January: 33.48%
February: 33.06%
March: 33.46%
Insight: Customer distribution remained almost evenly balanced across the three months, with January recording the highest share and February the lowest.

💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the dashboard findings, the following actions could be considered:
Revenue growth: Investigate the factors behind March's higher revenue and identify opportunities to sustain growth.
Customer segmentation: Explore the purchasing behavior of medium-income customers to understand their contribution to profitability.
Inventory planning: Review demand for white and gray products when making inventory decisions.
Brand strategy: Investigate the factors contributing to Apple's higher profit and identify opportunities to improve the profitability of other brands.
Customer retention: Maintain consistent customer engagement across all three months and investigate opportunities to increase the customer base.
These recommendations are based on the patterns visible in the dashboard and should be validated with additional data before implementation.
